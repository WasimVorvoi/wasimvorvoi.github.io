About the club

It's a real club for
building real companies.

Students form teams, pick a problem they actually care about, and spend the year turning it into a business with a pitch deck, a product, and a plan.

The curriculum comes straight from MIT. Your mentors have actually started companies, the competitions put real money on the table, and on pitch day you're in front of outside judges — not a practice panel.

Meetings are in the DLC (3rd Floor Annex), every other Tuesday until 4:00 PM.

01

Build

Form a team, build a first version, and see if people actually want it.

02

Pitch

Practice in front of mentors before real judges.

03

Compete

Enter 6 competitions across the school year.

The big one

LaunchSB.

Our end-of-year showcase. Every team pitches their business to a panel of judges. It's the whole point.

April 11 Saturday · 2026
Time
8:50 AM – 1:00 PM
Where
Annex Cafeteria
Required for club credit

"It's the whole year, condensed into one Saturday morning. Wear something nice."

Bring this stuff

— don't forget your charger.
  • Laptop & charger
  • Team poster board
  • Slide deck (ready to present)
  • Demo materials, if you have any
  • Business professional attire (see right →)

Dress code

— business / business formal.
  • Suits, blazers, dress shirts
  • Dress pants, skirts, blouses, dresses
  • Formal shoes (please, no sneakers)

Can't make it? Email us early — southbrunswickxstem@gmail.com. Just remember LaunchSB is required for credit, so reach out before the day-of.
