§ 07 — Workflow

How a session runs.

01QUERY

State what you want.

"Master's in mechanical engineering, English-taught, total cost under $40k/yr, climate temperate, English-speaking weekends preferred." Pro parses the brief.

02FILTER

14,820 → 148.

The corpus narrows to viable candidates in under a second. Each retained school carries its match reasons — which of your constraints it satisfies, which it bends.

03RANK

Your axis, your weights.

Default rank is by best-fit. Drag the weight sliders to re-rank by cost, climate, employment outcome, or any combination. The list refreshes in place.

04SHORTLIST

Pin a shortlist.

Drag your favorites into a compare board (up to 12). Every shortlist lives forever, syncs across devices, and exports as a tidy PDF for advisors or family.

05TRACK

Wake on change.

Pinned school raises tuition? Adds an English-taught track? Drops a visa requirement? Pro pings you the same business day, with the diff and the citation.
