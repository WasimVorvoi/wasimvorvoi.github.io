A reader's glossary.

Every funding body has its own vocabulary. Here is ours.
Fulbright U.S.
U.S. State Department program offering grants for graduate study, research, and English teaching abroad. Highly competitive; covers tuition, stipend, travel, and health.
Chevening U.K.
U.K. government's international scholarships programme funding one-year master's degrees at any U.K. university for emerging leaders from over 160 countries.
DAAD Germany
Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst — Germany's joint organisation funding international academic exchange at all levels. Famous for tuition-free German universities.
Erasmus Mundus EU
European Union's flagship joint master's programme. Students study at two or more European universities and receive full tuition, monthly stipend, travel, and insurance.
MEXT Japan
Japanese Government Scholarship from the Ministry of Education. Covers full tuition at Japanese universities plus a monthly living allowance for research and undergraduate students.
Commonwealth U.K.
Funded by the U.K.'s FCDO; supports talented individuals from low- and middle-income Commonwealth countries to study in the U.K. for one year.
Schwarzman China
Master's programme at Tsinghua University in Beijing, fully funded. Designed to prepare future leaders for the geopolitical challenges of the 21st century.
Rhodes U.K.
Oldest and one of the most prestigious international graduate scholarships, funding postgraduate study at the University of Oxford. Highly selective and life-changing.
