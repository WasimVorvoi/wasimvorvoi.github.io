§ 02 — Roadmap

The CEFR ladder.

The Common European Framework of Reference is the most widely-used measure of language ability. Six levels from absolute beginner to mastery — with rough hour estimates for the average English-speaking adult.

A1
Breakthrough
~80 hours
Order food. Introduce yourself. Read signs.
A2
Waystage
~180 hours
Survive a tourist trip. Talk about your day in past tense.
B1
Threshold
~350 hours
Hold a conversation. Watch TV with subtitles. Argue weakly.
B2
Vantage
~600 hours
Work in the language. Read novels slowly. Defend opinions.
C1
Effective
~1,000 hours
Native-comfortable. Write academic essays. Read literature.
C2
Mastery
~1,800 hours
Indistinguishable from an educated native, on a good day.

§ 05 — Methodology

Six schools of thought.

There is no single best way to learn a language. There are, however, six well-documented traditions — each with its own merits, weaknesses, and ideal use case.

I.
Comprehensible Input
Krashen's hypothesis: acquire language by understanding messages slightly above your level. Watch, read, listen — speak when ready.
Best for · Tonal languages · Listening
II.
Grammar-Translation
The classical method. Memorize rules, conjugate tables, translate sentences. Old-fashioned, but no faster way to read Cicero.
Best for · Reading literature · Latin/Greek
III.
Audiolingual / Pimsleur
Drill-based listen-and-repeat with spaced repetition baked in. Fastest path to a passable accent and basic conversation.
Best for · Travel · First 3 months
IV.
Assimil "With Ease"
French method built on natural dialogues, side-by-side translation, and a passive-then-active two-wave approach. Beloved cult favourite.
Best for · Self-study · Romance languages
V.
Spaced Repetition (SRS)
Anki, Memrise, FSRS. Algorithmic flashcards that schedule reviews at expanding intervals. Most efficient way to retain vocabulary long-term.
Best for · Vocab · Kanji · Characters
VI.
Immersion
Move, live, work in the language. The only method with a 100% historical track record — at the cost of being the most expensive and least scalable.
Best for · Fluency · Cultural depth
