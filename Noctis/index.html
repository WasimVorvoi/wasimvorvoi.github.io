§ 02 / The object
Four materials, one object

Hand-blown. Hand-finished. Built to outlast the trend.

One knurled brass ring is the only control — twist it to set your wake time, press it to dim. There is nothing to read, nothing to tap through, nothing to charge an opinion about at 6 a.m.

i — Shade
Opal glass

A 140 mm globe of hand-blown opal borosilicate that scatters light evenly — no hotspots, no glare.

ii — Base
FSC walnut

A turned base of solid, responsibly-sourced walnut that warms in tone as it ages with you.

iii — Collar
Brushed brass

A single knurled brass ring. Twist to set, press to dim. It patinas; it never needs an update.

iv — Voice
Tuning-fork chime

A struck brass tone instead of a buzzer — a sound you can wake to without flinching.

§ 03 / How it lives
Quiet by design

Quiet by design, day and night.

Five small decisions that disappear into the routine — so the lamp itself becomes invisible, and only the morning remains.

i Sunrise & sunset A 30-minute warm ramp up in the morning; a slow fade to ember at night to cue your body toward sleep. 30 min
ii Tap to snooze The glass itself is the snooze button. A single touch buys nine more minutes; the light dims, then returns. +9 min
iii Ambient-aware An onboard light sensor matches the room — dim in the dark, never harsh, never a beacon at 3 a.m. 0.1 lx
iv Eighteen-hour cordless Lift it off the magnetic puck and carry it anywhere. USB-C tops it up; a full charge lasts the night and then some. 18 hr
v App optional, never required Everything works from the ring. The app exists for routines and nothing more — and it can stay uninstalled. 0 KB
A note from the studio
We made Noctis because we were tired of being startled awake by rectangles. There is enough screen in a day; there should be none in the last and first ten minutes of it. So we built a sunrise you can keep on your nightstand — and asked it to do nothing else.
— The studio · Studio note 01 · Spring 2026
